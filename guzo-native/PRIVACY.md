# Guzo Fit — Privacy Policy

_Last updated with app version 1.2.0._

Guzo Fit stores everything on your device and sends nothing anywhere.

## What is collected

Nothing is collected. There is no account, no sign-up, no server, and no
backend. Your training history, bodyweight, food log, readiness check-ins,
routines and preferences are stored on the device only.

## What is transmitted

Nothing. The app makes no network requests other than loading its own files.
There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, and no
third-party SDK that transmits anything.

The one time you leave the app is when you tap to search for a form video,
which opens a YouTube search in your browser. That is you visiting YouTube
under their privacy policy; Guzo sends them nothing about you.

## Health data

If you grant access to Apple Health, Guzo reads sleep duration in order to
size your training session, and step count if you allow it. That data is used
on the device, is never transmitted, is never used for advertising, and is
never shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time in the
Health app. Guzo does not write to Apple Health.

## Your control

Export writes a JSON file of everything to wherever you choose. Restore
replaces what is on the device. Reset erases it. Deleting the app removes all
of it.

## Not medical advice

Calorie and protein targets are estimates from published equations and carry
their error bars on screen. The food library holds generic reference foods,
not products with labels, and is not an allergen database. Nothing in the app
is medical advice.

## Contact

The developer is the only person with any access, and that access is your own
device.
